Hello, I am writing to introduce RallyPack, an emergency preparedness resource for households, organizations, and businesses. Businesses can also keep preparedness planning and compliance records organized, including first aid kits, AEDs, staff certifications, and fire equipment checks, with reminders before anything expires.
